How Social Coffee Works

1

Scan QR Code

Every coffee shop, cafe, or social venue has a unique QR code. Scan it when you arrive to join that location's temporary community and discover who else is there.

2

Set Your Vibe

Choose what kind of connections you're looking for: networking, friendship, study buddy, or just casual coffee chat. Set your interests and availability.

3

Match & Connect

Get matched with like-minded people who are physically present at the same location. Start conversations, share experiences, make genuine connections over coffee.

4

Leave & Reset

When you leave the coffee shop, you're automatically removed from all conversations. Messages disappear, matches end, ensuring complete privacy and encouraging real-world follow-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does location verification work?
We use a combination of QR code scanning and GPS verification to ensure you're actually at the location. The QR code acts as the primary verification method, with GPS as a backup to prevent spoofing.
What happens to my data when I leave?
All conversations, matches, and location-specific data are permanently deleted when you leave a venue. We don't store chat histories or maintain permanent profiles tied to specific locations.
Can I reconnect with someone after leaving?
No, that's the point! TempMatch encourages real-world connections. If you want to stay in touch, exchange contact information while you're both present. This creates more meaningful, intentional connections.
How do you prevent fake profiles or harassment?
Since users must be physically present to participate, fake profiles are naturally limited. We also have robust reporting systems and can ban users from specific venues or the entire platform.
What types of venues will have QR codes?
We're starting with cafes, co-working spaces, bars, and networking events. We'll expand to gyms, libraries, parks, conferences, and any location where people might want to connect socially.
Is TempMatch free to use?
Basic features are completely free. Premium features like extended chat time, advanced filters, and business card sharing will be available through subscription tiers.
